# Enterprise Risk Management & Financial Stress Testing

## A Reproducible Case Study of Banco Santander

This repository presents an academic and reproducible analysis of Banco Santander's enterprise risk profile using publicly available financial information.

The project integrates enterprise risk management, financial risk analysis, stress testing, scenario analysis and risk-based decision making using Python.

## Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze Banco Santander's financial and risk profile.
- Identify the main financial and non-financial risks.
- Evaluate risk concentration and emerging vulnerabilities.
- Develop credit and capital stress-testing models.
- Analyze IFRS 9 Stage 1, Stage 2 and Stage 3 migration dynamics.
- Assess the potential impact of adverse scenarios on profitability, capital and liquidity.
- Build a reproducible risk-management framework using Python.

## Risk Areas

The analysis will progressively cover:

- Credit Risk
- Market Risk
- Liquidity Risk
- Interest Rate Risk in the Banking Book (IRRBB)
- Capital Adequacy
- Operational Risk
- Cyber and Technology Risk
- Model Risk
- Compliance and Conduct Risk
- Strategic Risk
- Emerging Risks
